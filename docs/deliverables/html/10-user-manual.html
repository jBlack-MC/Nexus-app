1Before you start

  • You need an Android phone or emulator running Android 7.0 or later, with the Nexus app installed from a build provided by the team. No app-store listing exists.
  • Your first registration or sign-in must happen while the device can reach the Nexus server. After that you can keep working offline.
  • Nexus has one kind of user. Everything you create is private to your account, and there are no shared projects.

Screen labels in this manual are the English ones. If you choose isiZulu or Setswana, the sign-in screens and some labels change language; most other screens stay in English.

2Account

2.1Create an account (UM-01)

  1. Open Nexus and wait for the intro animation to finish. The Login screen opens.
  2. Optional: choose your language in the Language box.
  3. Tap Need an account? Register.
  4. Enter Display Name, Email and a Password of at least 6 characters, then tap Create Account.
  5. The Today screen opens. You stay signed in when you close and reopen the app (for up to 24 hours per sign-in).

If you see "Something went wrong. Please try again." when registering, the email may already be registered, or you made too many attempts in a short time. Try signing in instead, or wait 15 minutes.

2.2Sign in and sign out (UM-02)

  1. On Login, enter your Email and Password and tap Sign In.
  2. To sign out, tap Log Out on the Today screen or in Settings, then confirm.

If a wrong password is entered, the app currently says "Your session has expired. Please log in again." Check your password and try again. This message is a known issue (AUD-18).

Changes you saved while offline stay on the device after you sign out. They upload the next time you sign in on that device. Another person signing in on the same phone will not see them.

3Today screen

The Today tab shows how many projects and tasks you have and how many tasks you've completed ("Activity"). Under Your schedule are three cards:

  • Overdue: unfinished tasks with a due date before today.
  • Due today: unfinished tasks due today.
  • Upcoming: the next three unfinished tasks with a future due date.

Tasks without a due date do not appear in these cards. "Showing saved data" means the numbers come from your device because the server could not be reached.

4Projects and tasks

4.1Create and manage a project (UM-03)

  1. Tap the Projects tab, then the + button.
  2. Enter a Name and an optional description, then confirm.
  3. Use Search projects to filter the list by name.
  4. Tap a project to open it. There you can rename it (Save Project), add tasks, open the task list (Open Tasks Screen) or Delete Project.

Deleting a project also deletes all its tasks. List deletes do not ask for confirmation, and there is no undo.

4.2Add and edit tasks (UM-04)

  1. In a project's task list, tap + (New Task).
  2. Enter a Title. Optionally add a description, a Due date (calendar picker; the × clears it), Priority (None, Low, Medium, High) and Status (To do, In progress, Done).
  3. To add labels, type in Add label and tap Add; tap × on a label to remove it. Add checklist steps the same way with Add step.
  4. Save. Tap a task to see its details, then Edit task to change it. Setting the status to Done marks the task complete.

4.3Find tasks (UM-05)

  • Search tasks or labels matches titles and labels.
  • Status chips: All, Open, In progress, Done. Tap High priority to show only high-priority tasks.
  • The bar at the top shows "x of y tasks complete".

5Habits

5.1Track a habit (UM-06)

  1. Tap the Habits tab, then + (Add habit).
  2. Enter a Habit name, choose Daily, Weekly or Custom, and tap Create.
  3. Each day you do the habit, tap Complete today. Tap it again (it reads Completed today) to undo.
  4. Use the pencil icon to edit, or the delete icon to remove a habit.

The stats card shows your level, points, current and best streak (in days), completion rate and badges ("3-day streak", "7-day streak", "Reliable habit builder"). With more than one habit, the completion rate shown is lower than your real rate (AUD-17).

6Settings

6.1Change your profile and preferences (UM-07)

  • Display Name: edit it in the User Profile section and tap Save Changes. Your email cannot be changed.
  • Appearance: System, Light or Dark. This applies to this device only.
  • Language: English, isiZulu or Setswana. It is saved to your account.
  • Task due-date reminders: this switch is saved, but Nexus does not send reminders yet (AUD-10).
  • Sync account reloads your profile from the server.

6.2Export your habit history (UM-08)

  1. In Settings, tap Export habits CSV, then Share CSV.
  2. Choose an app (email, Drive, etc.). The file lists habit, frequency and each completed date. Avoid commas in habit names; they break the CSV columns (AUD-11).

6.3Change password or delete your account (UM-09)

  • Change password: enter your current password and a new one (6+ characters), then tap Save. You must be online.
  • Delete account and data: confirm Delete. Your account and everything in it are permanently removed from the server and this device. This cannot be undone. You must be online.

7Working offline

7.1Understand the sync banner (UM-10)

The banner at the top of every screen tells you where your changes are:

Sync banner messages
MessageMeaningWhat to do
Saved on device · waiting to sync (n pending changes)Your change is safe on the phone and not yet on the server.Nothing. It uploads automatically when you're online, or tap Sync.
All changes syncedThe server has everything.Nothing.
Offline · changes saved on device / Offline · showing saved dataThe server can't be reached.Keep working; reconnect later.
Sign in again to sync saved changesYour session expired.Tap Sign in. Your saved changes are kept.
An item changed on another device · review requiredThe server rejected one change, for example because you edited an item that was deleted elsewhere.See UM-11.

Uploads happen while Nexus is open. If Android closes the app, uploading resumes when you open it again. Items you never opened or refreshed while online won't be available offline.

7.2Resolve a conflict (UM-11)

  1. Tap Review on the banner.
  2. Choose Discard item edits to drop your unsent changes to that one item. Your other changes then upload. Or choose Keep changes to decide later.
  3. Refresh the screen to load the server's current copy.

Until you discard the conflicting item, none of your other pending changes upload (AUD-19). They stay safely on your device.
