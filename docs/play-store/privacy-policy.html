Nexus privacy policy

Effective date: 5 November 2026 · Applies to the Nexus Android app (package com.example.nexus) and the Nexus API.

Before publishing: replace CONTACT_EMAIL below with the team's real contact address, and replace the package name if it is changed for Play Store release.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, display name and password. Passwords are stored only as a bcrypt hash. If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account ID, email address and name from Google.
  • Your content: the projects, tasks, labels, checklists and habits you create.
  • Preferences: language and notification settings. The theme setting stays on your device only.
  • Notification token: if push notifications are enabled, a Firebase Cloud Messaging token for your device, used only to send notifications to that device.

We do not collect location, contacts, photos, advertising IDs or analytics, and the app contains no ads.

How we use it

We use your data only to run Nexus: to sign you in, sync your content between your devices, show reminders and real-time updates, and display the app in your chosen language.

Where it is stored

Your content is saved on your phone so the app works offline, and on the Nexus API server once it syncs. On the phone, your sign-in token is stored encrypted. Your cached content and settings are excluded from Android cloud backups. All traffic between the app and the server uses HTTPS.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your data. Google provides sign-in (Google sign-in) and notification delivery (Firebase Cloud Messaging) as service providers.

Your choices and deletion

You can edit your profile, turn notifications off, and export your habit history at any time. Settings → Delete account and data permanently deletes your account and all of its content from the server and from your phone.

Retention

We keep your data until you delete it or your account.
